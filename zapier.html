Text Blaze + Zapier

Four demos. Open a page, click into a text box and type the shortcut. Each snippet runs a Zap through a webhook.

  1. 1

    Capture data from the current page and pass it to a Zap

    An order form open as a document. The snippet reads the customer and order details off the page, lets you review them, and sends them to a Zap.

    Open the ordertype z.order in the Notes box
  2. 2

    Turn interactions into Zaps

    A chat where a task comes up, then a meeting. One snippet logs the task, another schedules the meeting. Both trigger a Zap.

    Open the chattype z.task, send, then z.mtg
  3. 3

    Fill forms with data from Zapier

    A new customer form. The snippet triggers a Zap, the Zap sends the customer's details back, and the snippet fills the form.

    Open the formtype z.fill in First name
  4. 4

    Draft ad-hoc messages

    A customer question in chat. The snippet reads the question, takes a few inputs, and a Zap drafts the reply. You edit the draft, then insert it.

    Open the chattype z.draft in the message box
